<p>Oroboro dropped anchor in the very same place where 500+ years ago Pedro Álvares Cabral set foot on Brazil for the first time.&nbsp; The Bay is protected by a reef that is totally covered at high tide but nonetheless offers a very good shelter from winds from northeast and southeast.</p>
<img src="https://pub-7f7d07c430fd4c3eb11a4e6eae938ce3.r2.dev/318de927-66c0-45ee-ae51-c39627ed73d8-1.jpg" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-2958" alt="Corao Vermelha" width="1600" height="1200">
<p>There is a huge cross in stainless steel to commemorate the event.</p>
<img src="https://pub-7f7d07c430fd4c3eb11a4e6eae938ce3.r2.dev/img_4982.jpg" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-2962" alt="Corao Vermelha cross" width="3024" height="4032">
<p>But that's all there is under an historical point of view.&nbsp; The beach, however, is fantastic.&nbsp; It's a perfect location for kite surfing, but surprisingly there were none.&nbsp; So Yuka was very happy to have her second kitesurfing session in Brazil, after the one in Cabo Frio.</p>
<img src="https://pub-7f7d07c430fd4c3eb11a4e6eae938ce3.r2.dev/91a3ca52-7eb3-4766-a7c1-094c22e6211f-1.jpg" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-2959" alt="Kiteboarding in Corao Vermelha" width="1600" height="1200">
<p>The beach is also full of bars and restaurant.&nbsp; But what's more interesting is all kind of food vendors passing by.&nbsp; So you end up eating food from them, in whatever order they will come.&nbsp; Here Joaquin and Monica are having a dozen oysters.</p>
<img src="https://pub-7f7d07c430fd4c3eb11a4e6eae938ce3.r2.dev/img_4971.jpg" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-2965" alt="Oysters in Corao Vermelha" width="4032" height="3024">
<p>The other attraction of the beach was this guy offering tandem rides on his powered paraglider (Italian made).&nbsp; Monica being a skydiver enthusiast, could not pass on the offer.</p>
<img src="https://pub-7f7d07c430fd4c3eb11a4e6eae938ce3.r2.dev/img_4958.jpg" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-2966" alt="Power hand gliding in Corao Vermelha" width="4032" height="3024">
<p>So what do you during a windless day, after you have one too many caipirinhas?</p>
<img src="https://pub-7f7d07c430fd4c3eb11a4e6eae938ce3.r2.dev/img_5016.jpg" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-2967" alt="Going natives" width="3227" height="2731">
<p>Since a cold front was about to come, at some point we decided to set sails up north, and drop the hook in Santo Andrè, also know as Villa do 7 a 1.&nbsp; I'll explain why in the next post.</p>
