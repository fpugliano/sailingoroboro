<p>Year 2020, the Annus Horribilis (Latin phrase meaning "horrible year") is coming to an end, and it's now time for a year end review.</p>
<p>Let's start with counting our blessings:<br>
<ol><br>
 	<li>None of our family and friends caught Covid-19.  We only have one friend who was an asymptomatic positive and he's now totally fine.</li><br>
 	<li>We didn't catch Covid-19.  It would have been a disaster, because in this part of the world all of these island nations have maybe 2 or 3 ICU each.</li><br>
 	<li>We spent the Lock Down kite boarding in Barbuda.</li><br>
 	<li>We made new friends.</li><br>
 	<li>None of the 30 named tropical storms, 13 of which become hurricanes, made landfall in the Caribbean (although Gonzalo gave us a hard time).</li><br>
 	<li>Donald Trump lost the elections, so we can fly our American flag with pride again.</li><br>
 	<li>We sailed 1,500 nautical miles (we would have sailed more if it weren't because of covid).</li><br>
 	<li>I learnt how to jibe on my foil and Yuka learnt the Back Roll.</li><br>
 	<li>We did several improvements to the boat.</li><br>
 	<li>We visited 20 different islands in the Caribbean.</li><br>
</ol><br>
&nbsp;</p>
