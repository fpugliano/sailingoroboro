<p><strong>Guarapari, April 2019</strong></p>


<p>Guarapari was just a technical stop on our way to the Abrolhos archipelago.  Nice town.</p>
<figure style="margin:1.5rem 0;text-align:center;">
<img class="aligncenter wp-image-2655 size-full" src="https://pub-7f7d07c430fd4c3eb11a4e6eae938ce3.r2.dev/img_2204.jpg" alt="Guarapari, Brazil" width="2260" height="1638" />
<figcaption style="font-size:0.82rem;color:#666;margin-top:0.5rem;">Photo 1 · Guarapari, Brazil · 13 April 2019<br>Guarapari, Brazil</figcaption>
</figure>
<p>And on the way there we caught a nice Mahi mahi that Yuka cooked to perfection.</p>
<figure style="margin:1.5rem 0;text-align:center;">
<img class="aligncenter wp-image-2672 size-full" src="https://pub-7f7d07c430fd4c3eb11a4e6eae938ce3.r2.dev/img_4394.jpg" alt="Mahi Mahi in Brazil" width="2902" height="3881" />
<figcaption style="font-size:0.82rem;color:#666;margin-top:0.5rem;">Photo 2 · Guarapari, Brazil · 12 April 2019<br>Mahi Mahi in Brazil</figcaption>
</figure>
<p>We anchored in front of a gated community with beautiful villas along a natural reserve, where wealthy people from Vitoria have their vacation houses. Mostly coffee exporters, I heard.</p>
<figure style="margin:1.5rem 0;text-align:center;">
<img class="aligncenter wp-image-2657 size-full" src="https://pub-7f7d07c430fd4c3eb11a4e6eae938ce3.r2.dev/img_2219.jpg" alt="Gated community in Guarapari" width="4032" height="3024" />
<figcaption style="font-size:0.82rem;color:#666;margin-top:0.5rem;">Photo 3 · Guarapari, Brazil · 14 April 2019<br>Gated community in Guarapari</figcaption>
</figure>
<p>We felt very welcomed by the people living there, and the guards let us land with the dinghy on the beach and take a Uber to go downtown and visit the only monument that survived in town:  Igreja de Nossa Senhora da Concei&ccedil;&atilde;o.</p>

<p>The town is very new with the usual tall buildings along the beach, a nice waterfront but not much more.  It was a Saturday, and it was interesting to watch people enjoying the beach.</p>

<p>Time to get to Vitoria, another technical stop on the way to the Abrolhos archipelago!</p>
